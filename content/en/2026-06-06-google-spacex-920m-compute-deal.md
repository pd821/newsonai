# Google to Pay SpaceX $920 Million Per Month for AI Compute Resources

*TechCrunch reports massive infrastructure deal as AI demand surges*

**June 6, 2026** — Google has signed a landmark agreement to pay SpaceX approximately $920 million per month for compute resources, according to a TechCrunch report published Friday.

The deal, described by a Google representative as a response to "unexpected demand for its recently launched AI products," represents one of the largest single infrastructure commitments in the technology industry's history.

## Massive AI Infrastructure Push

The agreement comes as major technology companies race to secure enough computing power to train and run large AI models. Google has been aggressively expanding its AI capabilities, with recent product launches driving unprecedented demand for GPU clusters and cloud resources.

SpaceX, through its Starlink satellite network and associated data center operations, has emerged as an unexpected player in the AI infrastructure market. The company's extensive launch capabilities and rapidly expanding satellite constellation have enabled it to build significant ground-station and compute infrastructure.

## Industry Implications

The deal signals the escalating costs of AI development and the desperation of major tech firms to secure compute resources. With NVIDIA GPUs and cloud compute capacity in high demand, companies are exploring creative partnerships to access necessary resources.

"This represents a fundamental shift in how tech companies think about compute procurement," said one industry analyst familiar with the deal. "We're seeing agreements that would have seemed unimaginable just two years ago."

## Competition Intensifies

The Google-SpaceX partnership puts pressure on other major cloud providers, including Amazon Web Services and Microsoft Azure, to secure similar deals. The competition for AI compute resources has driven prices upward and created new partnerships between companies that would have been unlikely competitors just years ago.
