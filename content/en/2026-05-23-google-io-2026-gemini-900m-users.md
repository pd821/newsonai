Ten years ago, Google made a quiet but consequential shift: AI-first would become the company's guiding mandate. At Google I/O 2026 on Tuesday, CEO Sundar Pichai laid out what that bet has produced — and the numbers are striking.

Gemini, Google's flagship AI model and app, now serves **900 million monthly active users**, up from 400 million a year ago. Daily requests have grown more than sevenfold over the same period. The Gemini app crossed the half-billion-user threshold only earlier this year.

## The Numbers Behind the Moment

The scale Pichai described is hard to contextualize. Google's APIs now process approximately **19 billion tokens per minute**. Token throughput across the company's AI infrastructure has reached **3.2 quadrillion tokens per month** — a 7x increase year-over-year from roughly 480 trillion, and up dramatically from 9.7 trillion just two years ago.

More than **8.5 million developers** build with Google models each month. Among Google Cloud customers, **375** processed more than one trillion tokens over the past twelve months — a benchmark that signals serious enterprise adoption, not just consumer experimentation.

"We're now in the part of the AI cycle where people want to see the value in the products they use every day," Pichai told the keynote audience.

## Products at Scale

The numbers extend beyond Gemini. Thirteen Google products now have more than one billion users each; five of those surpass three billion users. That distribution matters: AI features are being woven into services people already rely on at enormous scale, rather than requiring them to adopt something new.

**AI Mode in Search** — Google's AI-native answer layer — crossed one billion monthly active users in roughly one year, a trajectory Pichai called "the biggest upgrade to Search ever." AI Overviews, which places AI-generated summaries at the top of search results, reaches **2.5 billion monthly active users**.

The message is consistent: agentic AI, systems that can reason across steps and take action on a user's behalf, is not a future promise. It is the present operating layer across Google's most trafficked products.

## What "Agentic" Means in Practice

Google has been careful not to overload the term, but at I/O 2026 the company made the agentic framing explicit and concrete. Gemini is increasingly positioned not just as a chatbot or a model but as a runtime — a substrate through which tasks get planned, executed, and iterated across services.

For developers, that means tools designed to let AI agents operate within Google's ecosystem rather than just generate text. For users, it means features like contextual memory, multi-step task completion, and tighter integration with Calendar, Drive, Maps, and other Google services.

Pichai made no mention of a specific new model name or dramatic capability reveal. Instead, the keynote leaned into what Google calls "the agentic Gemini era" — a framing that treats agents as a deployment reality rather than a research milestone.

## Context and Caution

The growth metrics are large and directionally consistent with the broader industry trajectory. OpenAI, Anthropic, and Microsoft have all reported significant increases in API usage and user bases over the same period. Google's advantage — if it holds — is distribution: existing products with billions of users create a frictionless on-ramp to AI features that standalone AI companies must build from scratch.

The question observers will be tracking: whether usage growth translates to revenue growth, and whether the enterprise customers processing trillions of tokens represent sustainable business rather than early-stage experimentation. Google has not broken out AI-specific revenue at the level of detail that would settle that question.

## What's Next

Google is expected to detail developer-facing agentic toolkits and updated API pricing tiers in the days following I/O. The company signaled it will continue expanding what Gemini can do within Search, Assistant, and Workspace — areas where the agentic model meets Google's most active user bases.

For now, the keynote delivered a clear message: the infrastructure is built, the users are present, and the agentic era is no longer a thesis — it's a product tier.
