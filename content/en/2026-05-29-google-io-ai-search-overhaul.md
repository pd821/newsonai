Google has officially retired the era of the 10 blue links.

At its I/O 2026 developer conference, the tech giant unveiled a sweeping AI-driven overhaul of Google Search, powered by the new Gemini 3.5 Flash model. The update brings conversational AI queries, autonomous agents, and AI-generated answers front and center — a departure so dramatic that one industry headline called it "the biggest transformation of Search in 25 years."

For brands and publishers who built their traffic strategies around traditional search engine optimization, the implications are significant. AI answers now appear before traditional web listings, raising questions about visibility and discoverability.

"Search queries hit an all-time high last quarter," said Google in its blog post announcing the changes. "Today, we're sharing the next step — bringing together the best of a search engine with the best of AI."

Among the headline features: AI agents that can complete tasks on behalf of users, smart glasses developed with Android XR partners, and a new conversational search mode. Google also introduced "Ask YouTube" and deepened AI integration across productivity tools.

The shift marks Google's clearest answer yet to rivals like OpenAI and Perplexity, who have been gaining ground with AI-first search experiences. Whether Google's move will win back users — or further consolidate its dominance — remains to be seen.
