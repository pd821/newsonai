# India GDP Growth Surpasses 8%, Becomes World's Fastest-Major Economy

*June 4, 2026 — India's economy grew 8.1% in fiscal year 2026, cementing its position as the world's fastest-growing major economy and outpacing all G20 nations for the third consecutive year.*

## What Happened

India's economy grew 8.1% in fiscal year 2026 (April 2025-March 2026), cementing its position as the world's fastest-growing major economy. The milestone was announced by India's Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation on June 4, 2026.

The growth was driven by strong performance across manufacturing, digital services, infrastructure spending, and robust domestic consumption. The figures exceeded analyst expectations of 7.5% and marked the third consecutive year India has grown faster than any other G20 economy.

## Key Points

- **GDP Growth**: 8.1% in FY 2026 (April 2025 - March 2026)
- **Previous year**: 7.2% in FY 2025
- **Ranking**: Fastest-growing major economy for third consecutive year
- **Key drivers**: Manufacturing, digital services, infrastructure, domestic consumption

## Economic Breakdown

| Sector | Contribution | Growth Rate |
|--------|--------------|-------------|
| Manufacturing | 22% of GDP | +12.3% |
| Digital Services | 14% of GDP | +15.8% |
| Infrastructure | 8% of GDP | +18.2% |
| Agriculture | 18% of GDP | +3.1% |
| Financial Services | 12% of GDP | +9.4% |

## Why It Matters

India's economic trajectory positions it as a leading global growth engine alongside China. The country's young demographics, expanding digital infrastructure, and manufacturing boom have attracted significant foreign investment, with India recording $100 billion in foreign direct investment in FY2026.

Economists project India could become the world's third-largest economy by 2030, behind only the United States and China.

## Government Response

Prime Minister Modi's government has attributed the growth to structural reforms including:
- Production-Linked Incentive (PLI) schemes for manufacturing
- Digital India infrastructure expansion
- Infrastructure corridor development
- Financial inclusion initiatives

The finance ministry announced continued investment in these areas, with a record infrastructure budget for FY2027.

## Global Implications

India's growth is increasingly important for global supply chains as companies diversify away from China. Apple's iPhone production in India, for example, has tripled since 2024, making India the company's second-largest manufacturing base.
